How a Texas foreclosure auction works

Four steps

Texas mortgage foreclosures happen outside of court, at a live public auction run by a trustee. Here is the path from notice to keys.

  1. Notice is filedA notice of sale is posted with the County Clerk at least 21 days before the auction. It names the property, the sale date and the earliest start time.
  2. Do your homeworkPull the notice, order a title search, drive by the house, and set your bid ceiling. You cannot go inside or get an inspection.
  3. Sale dayFirst Tuesday of the month, between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. If that Tuesday is Jan 1 or July 4, the sale moves to Wednesday. Highest bidder wins.
  4. After you winYou pay the trustee and receive a trustee's deed. If the home is occupied, getting possession can mean an eviction case. Insure it right away.
RiskSold as-is, sight unseen

No interior access, no inspection, no seller disclosures. Budget repairs as if you will find surprises.

RiskSome liens stay with the house

The sale wipes out liens junior to the one being foreclosed. Senior liens and unpaid property taxes survive and become yours. A title search before bidding is the only way to know.

RiskSales get pulled

A payoff, a loan workout or a bankruptcy filing can cancel a sale right up to auction day. Re-check the filing the morning of the sale.

Good to knowMortgage sales are final

Texas gives the former owner no general right to buy the home back after a mortgage foreclosure sale.

RiskTax and HOA sales are different

After a tax sale the former owner can redeem for 2 years (homestead or farm land) or 180 days (other property). HOA foreclosures carry a 180-day redemption right.

RiskCash, fast

You generally cannot use a regular mortgage at the courthouse. Have funds ready and confirm the trustee's payment terms before sale day.

Rules come from Texas Property Code ch. 51 and the Dallas County Clerk. Redemption periods are summarized from this Texas attorney's overview. This is general information, so talk to a real estate attorney before you bid.
